# Copy reference
Most "premium" sites fail on words before they fail on pixels. Local customers search for a problem plus a place ("termite tenting Port St. Lucie") and decide in seconds whether you fix it and whether they can trust you.

## Hero
- **H1:** service + place, in the customer's words, in 10 words or fewer. Personality goes in the verb or the specificity, not in adjectives.
  - Weak: "Absolute Climate Mastery for South Florida Estates."
  - Strong: "AC repair in Boca Raton, usually same day."
  - Strong: "Termite tenting and pest control across Port St. Lucie."
- **Subline:** one line of concrete proof, 20 words or fewer: years, a guarantee, a number, a name. For example: "Family-run since 2006. If termites come back within a year, so do we, free."
- **Primary CTA:** `Call (772) 555-0148` as a `tel:` link. Name the action, not "Get started".
- **Secondary CTA:** "Text us a photo" (`sms:`) or "Book a free inspection".

## Sections
- **Services:** the name the customer uses, what's included, and when you need it. Add a price range if the client allows one, because it pre-qualifies leads and AI answers quote prices.
- **Proof:** real reviews, verbatim, with first name and town. Use the specific jobs, not "great service".
- **About:** who answers the phone, how long they've been doing this, and why they care. Use first person plural and short sentences.
- **FAQ:** the questions people actually ask on the phone. Each answer opens with a 40–60 word direct answer; this is what AI search lifts.
- **Close:** restate the offer and the phone number, plus the service area.

## Voice
- Write like the owner talking to a neighbour: plain, warm and specific.
- Use numbers over adjectives, and verbs over nouns.
- Cut every sentence that would fit any company in any town.

## Banned (they signal template copy)
elevate, unlock, seamless, cutting-edge, revolutionize, world-class, mastery, estates (unless it's literally an estate service), bespoke, excellence, solutions, "your trusted partner", "we are a family owned business dedicated to quality service", "Welcome to our website", "Click here", "in today's fast-paced world".

## Headings
Section headings need the same specificity as the H1. "What we do", "Our services", "About us" and "Why choose us" are template headings. Write "Five things we get called for" or "Who picks up the phone" instead.

## Placeholders
If a fact isn't sourced, write `[[NEEDS: Google review count]]` inline, visibly. Don't round, estimate or "illustrate" numbers on a real business's site.
